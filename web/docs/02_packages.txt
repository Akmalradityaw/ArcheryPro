C. KONFIGURASI TAILWIND + DAISYUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

File: tailwind.config.js

- colors.primary.light   = '#4CAF50'
- colors.primary.dark    = '#1B5E20'

- JANGAN pakai gradient, JANGAN pakai theme default DaisyUI yang colorful
- Plugins: @tailwindcss/forms, @tailwindcss/typography, daisyui

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. AUTH CUSTOM (TANPA BREEZE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kenapa custom?
- Breeze pakai struktur & views bawaan yang tidak sesuai spec
- Butuh registrasi multi-role dengan token berbeda (pelatih/scoring)
- Kontrol penuh atas layout & flow

Yang perlu dibuat manual:
- AuthController (login, logout, showLogin)
- RegisterController (registerAtlet, registerPelatih, registerScoring)
- Middleware: Authenticate (cek login), RedirectIfAuthenticated
- Middleware: RoleMiddleware (pakai spatie/laravel-permission)
- Blade: auth/login.blade.php, auth/register-atlet.blade.php,
         auth/register-pelatih.blade.php, auth/register-scoring.blade.php
- Guard: gunakan guard 'web' standar (session-based)
